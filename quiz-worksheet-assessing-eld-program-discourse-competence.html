After careful review of assessments in an ELD program, a team realizes that the assessments do give students lots of appropriate practice with speaking skills but lack practice for writing skills. The best option the team has is:

  1. to design a plan to develop more writing assessments

  2. to buy materials that focus on writing

  3. to modify program goals so writing is not a key goal

  4. to train teachers on how to assess writing skills

As part of developing the writing skills aspect of discourse competence in an ELD program, teachers would ideally modify their instruction to:

  1. get students to write as much as possible

  2. explicitly teach writing techniques

  3. focus on writing activities

  4. give more writing assessments

The evaluation of the skills for discourse competence in ELD programs requires this first step:

  1. teacher evaluation

  2. analysis of program goals

  3. modification of goals for the program

  4. comparison of results with program goals

The following three are key steps of ELD program evaluation:

  1. speaking, writing, and debate materials

  2. materials, standardized tests, and quizzes

  3. materials, assessment, and instructional practice

  4. review, correction, and implementation

Which of the following exercises on a textbook for ELD programs elicits discourse competence practice?

  1. Write ten sentences using adjectives.

  2. Tell your peers everything you remember about your last vacation.

  3. Orally conjugate the following verbs in the past tense.

  4. Write a list of 20 regular verbs in the past tense.
