What are some strategies for building background knowledge in English language learners?

  1. Agree or disagree statements

  2. Discussion of what students think they know about a subject matter before a lesson

  3. Visiting museums, attending festivals, pre-readings, discussing pictures

  4. True or false statements

What is background knowledge for ELL students?

  1. Any knowledge, experiences and beliefs students already have about the world

  2. A special discussion held after class to answer questions

  3. Any misconceptions a student may develop in the process of decoding new content

  4. Any explanation, definition or clarification of concepts that may faciliate student's comprehension of new material

What are two factors that impact language comprehension and therefore require background knowledge?

  1. Occupational and cultural differences

  2. Cultural and geographical differences

  3. Age and occupation differences

  4. Age and geographical differences

How does background knowledge differ from prior knowledge for ELL students?

  1. Background knowledge may contain misconceptions and misunderstandings as it comes from personal experience, while prior knowledge comes from the instructor, so it is accurate and factual.

  2. Background knowledge and prior knowledge are more or less the same thing.

  3. Background knowledge is less reliable than prior knowledge.

  4. Prior knowledge may contain misconceptions and misunderstandings as it comes from personal experience, while background knowledge comes from the instructor, so it is more likely to be accurate and factual.

What is prior knowledge for ELL students?

  1. A short paragraph of reading provided by the instructor to ease students into the new material for the day, as they may not understand certain concepts

  2. A student's takeaway from a lesson

  3. A student's personal, academic and cultural experience of the surrounding world, which helps the student connect new material to familiar concepts

  4. A student's misconceptions and misunderstandings about an issue
