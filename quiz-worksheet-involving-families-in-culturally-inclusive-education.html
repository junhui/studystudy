One key way to improve family and community involvement in your curriculum is to:

  1. Know that parents will come to you if they want to be involved.

  2. Work with the newspaper to advertise events.

  3. Give the students messages to deliver to their parents, such as event notices.

  4. Build relationships and be present at conferences, meetings, etc.
