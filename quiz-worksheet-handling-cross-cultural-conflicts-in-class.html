To successfully resolve a multicultural conflict, an instructor has to take the following steps:

  1. Listen carefully, identify cultural norms possibly causing the problem, respect both viewpoints, and encourage students to discuss and resolve while you mediate.

  2. Call students in front of everybody in class and tell them who is wrong.

  3. Hear student A and student B's arguments and determine who is right and who is wrong.

  4. Avoid bringing students together and discuss the issue on behalf of both parties.
