What does it mean to have an inclusive school?

  1. It means to analyze the visuals in the halls and classrooms.

  2. It means to ensure that everyone feels welcome at the school.

  3. It means to have a diverse student body.

  4. It means to have a school with implicit cultural values.

What are implicit cultural values?

  1. Values that are not articulated directly

  2. Values related to rules and discipline

  3. Values that focus on individualism

  4. Values that are stated clearly

What question might you ask about a teacher's pedagogy when thinking about implicit cultural values?

  1. What is your favorite subject to teach?

  2. What do you tell your students about diversity?

  3. How many students are in your class?

  4. What learning styles do you try to appeal to?

You can learn a lot about implicit cultural values in a school by focusing on the approach to _____.

  2. the mission statement

What might you ask yourself about a classroom when analyzing implicit cultural values?

  1. How many students can fit in the classroom comfortably?

  2. What color are the classroom walls?

  3. How are the students positioned in relation to a teacher?

  4. How many different books are available in the classroom?
