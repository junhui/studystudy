What is diversity?
  1. People of different ethnic backgrounds
  2. People with different abilities
  3. People with different sexual orientations
  4. Differences of all types
What should a teacher do if questions about diversity feel uncomfortable or seem too hard?
  1. If a question makes you uncomfortable, apologize to students for the fact that it arose.
  2. If a question makes you feel uncomfortable, trust your instincts about what you and your students can handle.
  3. If a question makes you feel uncomfortable, do not respond to it.
  4. If a question makes you feel uncomfortable, bring in your school social worker to facilitate.
What should a teacher do about racial diversity if all the students in a class are from one particular ethnic group?
  1. Do not address racial diversity in the classroom.
  2. Teach students only about types of diversity that do not pertain to race.
  3. Draw on external resources to help students talk and think about racial diversity.
  4. Give one lecture at the start of the school year on racial diversity.
How can making a chart that identifies different students' strengths help promote diversity in a classroom?
  1. It helps teachers identify whether a class consists mainly of achieving or non-achieving students who need help.
  2. It helps students appreciate the range of abilities in the class and identify classmates they can go to for help.
  3. None of the answers are correct; teachers should never make a public display of students' abilities.
  4. It helps students appreciate the range of abilities in the class and identify classmates they should emulate.
What is one way to have students appreciate their own identities?
  1. Have them look up the definition of diversity.
  2. Have them do self-portraits.
  3. Have them interview a classmate.
  4. Have them read newspaper articles.
