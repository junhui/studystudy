The term sheltered instruction means:
  1. ESLs who are struggling can be excused from their assignments if the teacher thinks it is too hard.
  2. ESL students are not required to learn the same curriculum as their peers.
  3. The curriculum is taught in a way that all students, including ESL students, can understand.
  4. ESLs learn a specialized curriculum that includes instruction in their native language.
ESL students can become more successful in the classroom by:
  1. Memorizing words or phrases that they will come across in the classroom.
  2. Going to year-round schools.
  3. Not being allowed to communicate in their native language while in school.
  4. Building background knowledge of vocabulary words and phrases.
Word walls are:
  1. Meaningless in vocabulary instruction.
  2. A great way to decorate the classroom.
  3. Designed to showcase student work samples.
  4. A great way to introduce vocabulary words.
For ESL students, the most critical part of instruction in any classroom is:
  1. Rote memorization
  2. Worksheets
  3. Vocabulary
  4. Pop quizzes
Scaffolding vocabulary instruction means:
  1. Instruction is broken down into smaller chunks.
  2. Curriculum and instruction is watered down so students who are struggling can learn.
  3. Students have to build their own knowledge base.
  4. Teachers are responsible for making instruction as challenging as possible.
