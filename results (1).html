What is the SIOP model?
Explanation
The SIOP model stands for Sheltered Instruction Observation Protocol and is a researched-based method of instruction that focuses on having English language learners (ELLs) learn alongside their native English-speaking peers, rather than being pulled out of the classroom for special English instruction.

Which of the following should NOT be done when the SIOP process is completed?
Explanation
Reflection of lesson effectiveness is important for determining what changes need to be made before a lesson is used again. Assessing should be done throughout the lesson, and again when the lesson is complete. Failure to do so, means crucial details will be forgotten and important changes will not be made. Complete reflections, reviews of vocabulary, feedback from students, and objectives' reviews thoroughly to properly note what modifications should be made before the lesson is put away.
