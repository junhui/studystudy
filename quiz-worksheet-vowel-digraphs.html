How is a vowel digraph different from a diphthong?
  1. A vowel digraph is two vowel letters which together represent a single vowel sound, while a diphthong is two vowel sounds which combine together.
  2. A vowel digraph and a diphthong are the same thing.
  3. A vowel digraph is two sounds spelled by one letter, while a diphthong is two letters.
  4. A vowel digraph is two vowel letters which spell a single sound, while a diphthong is two consonants.

Why is the strategy for teaching vowel digraphs described by the phrase 'when two vowels go walking the first one does the talking' sometimes problematic?
  1. Vowel digraphs are very rare in written English.
  2. It is nearly always the second letter which makes the sound of the vowel digraph.
  3. It is the first consonant that determines the sound in consonant digraphs.
  4. There are many exceptions to this generalization, like in the word chief.
What is a digraph?
  1. A digraph is a misspelled word.
  2. A digraph is two sounds together.
  3. A digraph is two letters which together represent a single sound.
  4. A digraph is a single letter.
One strategy often used to teach vowel digraphs to learning readers is to _____.
  1. have the learning reader find words that are incorrectly spelled
  2. group words containing different consonant digraphs like ch, th, and sh
  3. ask the learner to learn to read digraphs before simple single vowel letters
  4. group words containing different digraphs which all make the same sound, like cow and cloud, or rain and day
