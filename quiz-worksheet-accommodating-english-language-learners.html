  1. Allow the student to work quietly on something else. If they can't understand the directions, there is no point in taking the quiz.

  2. Read the directions and word problems aloud to the student in a quiet area of the classroom where other students won't be distracted.

  3. Allow the student to work independently on the assignment, but be available if they have any questions.

  4. Give the student access to the quiz key so they can copy the correct answers.
