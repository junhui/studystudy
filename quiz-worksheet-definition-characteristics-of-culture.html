What is a subculture?
  1. A culture that opposes another culture.
  2. Symbols and patterns of behavior that develop among a specific group within a larger culture.
  3. Another term for low culture; the culture produced by everyday people interacting in society.
  4. The dominant, primary culture of a society that is experienced by the majority of people.
What is material culture?
  1. The same thing as high culture.
  2. A type of culture as seen through intangible ideas and beliefs.
  3. The physical items produced by a culture.
  4. A subculture that is focused on building materials.

What is culture?
  1. A group of people who share things in common.
  2. A set of patterns of human activity within a society.
  3. A made-up word.
  4. A system of social division that organized people into different levels.
