What is a consonant blend?

  1. A combination of consonants together in a word, where each sound can be heard

  2. The sound a word makes when it begins and ends with consonants

  3. A sound made when consonants and vowels are combined

  4. A combination of consonants that form to make one, singular sound

How do you pronounce a consonant blend with two of the same letters?

  1. You pronounce the consonant blend as if only one of the letters was there.

  2. Neither letter is pronounced, and they are treated as silent letters.

  3. You pronounce each letter individually.
