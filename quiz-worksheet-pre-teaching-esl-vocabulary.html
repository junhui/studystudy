Worksheet: How To Pre-Teach Vocabulary to ESL Students

https://study.com/how-to-pre-teach-vocabulary-to-esl-students.html
Your student Ana does really well when she looks at visuals whereas Patricia excels when it is time to do listening. Which of the following vocabulary pre-teaching exercises would be the LEAST helpful?
  1. Pass each student a worksheet with the list of new vocabulary words.
  2. Tell students how each word is pronounced and then have them repeat after you.
  3. Spell each word on the board after saying it aloud.
  4. Create a PowerPoint with images for each labeled vocabulary word.
The following statement is NOT good advice in regards to pre-teaching vocabulary:
  1. If you pre-teach vocabulary, it is wise to choose a list of eight to ten words.
  2. When pre-teaching vocabulary, the best way is to present simple dictionary definitions.
  3. To pre-teach vocabulary, we should choose the words that our students really need.
  4. When we pre-teach vocabulary, the new words are out of context so we need to provide helpful clues.
You decide to use visual aids to pre-teach vocabulary. You tell your students the pronunciation of each word as they see the visual. The following is NOT an activity you can give your students right after:
  1. Show your students the spelling of each word
  2. Ask students to repeat aloud to check for pronunciation
  3. Ask students to spell the word
  4. Ask the whole class to repeat the word aloud
A good way to pre-teach vocabulary is by discussing new words through questions. Say you are teaching the word 'snowflake.' The following is NOT a good question to ask:
  1. During what season of the year do we see snowflakes?
  2. What part of speech is the word 'snowflake'?
  3. Why do snowflakes have six arms?
  4. Do you think a snowflake is pretty?
You decide to pre-teach vocabulary by giving your ESL students a 'fill in the blanks' exercise, with a list of the vocabulary words to choose from. The following is the ONE step you cannot skip before your students fill in the blanks:
  1. Show a visual of each word.
  2. Give a dictionary definition of each word.
  3. Practice spelling of each word.
  4. Give the pronunciation of each word.
