ELL students are writing a short composition on their favorite holiday. Leila, who is a Muslim ELL, writes about Ramadan and writes (peace be upon Him) when referring to Muhammad. This particular way of writing has to do with Leila's first language regarding:

  1. cultural expectations

  2. directionality

  3. sentence structure

  4. discourse structure

Which of the following ELLs who joins school in the US has the best advantage to develop writing skills in English?

  1. a third grade student who reads and writes Russian

  2. a third grade student who speaks Russian but does not write it

  3. an adult who recently learned to read and write in Spanish

  4. an illiterate student

The following is NOT a transferable skill between first language writing and English writing:

  1. ability to scan text

  2. ability to produce a conclusion

  3. ability to summarize

  4. ability to form correct sentence structure

Ahmed is an Arab student who always replaces the letter 'p' for a 'b'. For instance, he writes 'I am going to the bark' instead of 'the park'. This is probably due to an influence of Ahmed's first language in regards to:

  1. directionality

  2. discourse structure

  3. sentence structure

  4. orthographic depth

Pierre is a French speaking ELL who often skips the auxiliary verb to write questions. He says this type of verb is not used in French. Pierre's ELL teacher can do the following about this:

  1. remind Pierre this is a difference between English and French

  2. get Pierre to write 100 examples with the auxiliary 'do'

  3. ask Pierre to forget about French and stick to English rules

  4. talk to Pierre to reinforce the need to use the auxiliary 'do'
