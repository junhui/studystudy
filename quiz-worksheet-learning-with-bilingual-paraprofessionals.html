A SDAIE program is different from an ELD program because:

  1. the SDAIE program is more important than the ELD

  2. the SDAIE program covers academic content in English

  3. the SDAIE program is in English

  4. the SDAIE program is aimed to developing English language

Mario is a student from Venezuela who is in a SDAIE program. The bilingual professional in his class is there to play the role of:

  1. speaking in English to Mario

  2. teaching Mario in Spanish

  3. translating everything for Mario into Spanish

  4. assisting Mario and other students with the academic content

Claire is a bilingual paraprofessional who realizes that students are having a hard time understanding the teacher's instructions for a reading task. Claire is there to work along with the teacher, so her best practice approach in this situation is:

  1. repeat the instructions in English but slowly

  2. speak Spanish to the students to clarify the instructions

  3. talk to individual students to help them

  4. ask the teacher if she needs her to translate the instructions

Bilingual professionals are a great asset in schools because:

  1. they can serve as interpreters

  2. they can serve as translators

  3. all choices

  4. they can facilitate communication between parents and teachers

A bilingual person can work as an assistant in a classroom as long as they:

  1. have a paraprofessional certification

  2. have experience translating

  3. have experience working along with a teacher

  4. have experience working with children
