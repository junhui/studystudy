Who carries the most responsibility for creating an inclusive instructional environment in an ELL classroom?

  1. The school administrators

  2. The students

  3. The teacher

  4. The parents

Why is it important for ELL students to feel that their opportunities to use their native languages are equal among all students?

  1. If students don't feel native language use is equal, their assessment performances will suffer.

  2. If students feel there is inequality, they may become resentful of the teacher.

  3. If students feel they are being treated unfairly, they are more likely to ignore homework assignments.

  4. If students feel they are being treated unfairly, they will be more emboldened to cheat.

What can make ELL students more inclined to follow the rules of native language usage in class?

  1. If a school administrator observes the class to ensure compliance.

  2. If they are threatened with detention or other punishment.

  3. If the rules are clearly defined and consistently enforced.

  4. If they are told their scores will be lowered for each infraction.

How can group work help ELLs feel a sense of inclusion with their peers?

  1. Group work ensures student will make friends.

  2. They can hear different accents and opinions and work through communication issues together.

  3. Group work gives students a chance to plan on how to get the teacher to like them.

  4. Group work allows students to compare how good they are at English compared to others.

What is one reason young ELLs may be hesitant to speak in front of others?

  1. They are afraid other students will be jealous if they speak well.

  2. Young ELLs are usually not afraid to speak in class but older ELLs are.

  3. They are afraid the teacher will not like them if they make a mistake.

  4. They fear making vocabulary and pronunciation errors.
