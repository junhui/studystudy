For speakers of other languages in California, the following is a good strategy to apply so they develop their word analysis skills:

  1. recognize cognates

  2. find structures of words

  3. identify sounds of words

  4. practice reading aloud

A California teacher assigns students to find the words in an article that have the same sound as the 'e' in 'identify'. When students do this type of exercise, they develop their:

  1. decoding skills

  2. vocabulary skills

  3. phonemic awareness

  4. morphemic awareness

Word analysis is an important reading skill students should have because:

  1. not all students can translate words into speech

  2. some students cannot read fluently

  3. not all students are native speakers of English

  4. some students find reading to be a hard task

A student in California matches the words he hears to what is written, and he also identifies the name and author of a story. These tasks are part of the following skill:

  1. identifying letters

  2. applying concepts about print

  3. developing phonemic awareness

  4. translating printed words into speech

A student who is able to decode words is able to:

  1. quickly translate print into speech

  2. understand the meaning of the words in print

  3. read aloud regardless of mistakes

  4. translate print into speech with some difficulty
