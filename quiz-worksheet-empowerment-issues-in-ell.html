Worksheet: Empowerment Issues When Educating English Learners

https://study.com/empowerment-issues-when-educating-english-learners.html
A teacher who decorates the ELL classroom nicely creates a positive environment. However, a positive environment also requires the teacher to:

  1. get students to help with decorations.

  2. include decorations in ELLs native language.

  3. ensure students get good grades.

  4. promote respectful interactions between students.

It is the beginning of the year and a school in California sends welcoming letters to parents. To make sure parents of ELL students get the message, the school could:

  1. have an interpreter make a call about the message

  2. tell ELL students to orally tell their parents they are welcome

  3. not send the letter to ELL parents due to language barriers

  4. send bilingual letters

A school in California celebrates Halloween and ELLs participate. However, Mexican ELLs would like to wear some costumes that are typical of Día de los Muertos back in Mexico. The school should:

  1. encourage students to do so and to teach their culture.

  2. get students to explain to others about their costumes.

  3. allow but ask students to include a Halloween detail.

  4. ask the students to refrain from wearing their costumes.

Cristóbal is an ELL parent who wishes to opt out of the ELL program the school offers for his children. The school accepts this decision but should:

  1. document the decision and inform Cristóbal of all implications.

  2. ask Cristóbal to let the kids try the program for a month.

  3. ignore the decision for the sake of the children's progress.

  4. try to convince Cristóbal to reconsider his decision.

A school organizes an event with parents to clean a beach and raise environmental awareness in the community. The school should:

  1. include all parents regardless of language barriers.

  2. have a separate event for non-English speaking parents.

  3. pair non-English speaking parents with others like them.

  4. make sure children are there to interpret for their parents.
