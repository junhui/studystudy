Which of the following students is NOT being respectful?
  1. A student who makes eye contact with the teacher when the teacher is talking
  2. A student who addresses the teacher as Mr. Smith
  3. A student ignoring another student who is talking to them
  4. A student cleaning up after using shared classroom supplies
Which of the following is the BEST way to show students that the school and teacher truly respect them?

  1. By telling students what to do and when, without any input from them

  2. By allowing students to help draft important guidelines that ultimately affect them

  3. By focusing solely on one major religious holiday, one likely to affect most students at the school

  4. By letting the students do whatever they want

Ms. Zara is concerned about her student, Jill, who has shown clear disrespect towards her physical learning environment. Which of the following could be an action that led to Ms. Zara's concern?

  1. Jill has been rude and obstinate toward Ms. Zara.

  2. Jill was yelling at a classmate.

  3. Jill has been writing on her desk.

  4. Jill often turns away from classmates who are talking.

Which of the following is a TRUE statement about students and respect in school?

  1. Students are not knowledgeable enough to draft respect-based guidelines.

  2. Students are the only ones ultimately in charge of creating any respect-based guidelines.

  3. Students are unable to learn any respect within a classroom. They can only learn this at home.

  4. Students are often able to tell when a teacher is being truly respectful or play-acting.

Which of the following teachers is demonstrating respectful behavior towards her students?

  1. Ms. Wiley doesn't take time from her lecture to note students who walk in later. She continues talking, facing the board in order to draw attention to the notes there.

  2. Ms. Riley ignores the troublemakers in her class so that she can pay attention to the kids who are doing what they are supposed to.

  3. Ms. Jamison doesn't allow students to share their opinions on a topic and focuses on facts instead.

  4. Ms. Benson greets every student by name when they enter her classroom, looks at them, and smiles during the greeting.
