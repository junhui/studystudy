Why are student interviews ideal activities for introducing a new unit?
  1. the students can learn more about one another
  2. the teacher can gauge what students already know about the topic and how much experience they have with it
  3. interviews give students a break from doing difficult writing
  4. interviews cover some of the main themes of the text

What is one benefit of using talking chips during discussions?
  1. They give teachers control over who speaks during class discussions.
  2. They ensure that all students participate in the discussion.
  3. They assign one student in each group with the task of speaking for the whole group.
  4. They give quiet students an option to not participate during discussions.
