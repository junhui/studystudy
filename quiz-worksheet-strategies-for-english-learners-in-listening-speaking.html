Why is it important to be consistent with the listening and speaking strategies you use with English learners?

  1. You should NOT be consistent, to make sure the students understand each exception to a rule

  2. It will help students develop good academic habits.

  3. Consistency is the only way that students will learn proper English.

As a teacher of English learners, why can it be helpful to review the key vocabulary after a lesson that you had previewed before the lesson?

  1. It allows students to check their understanding and listening skills.

  2. It ensures all students will remember and use those words correctly in the future.

  3. This type of review will guarantee higher test scores.

  4. It is only helpful if it's an English lesson and not a science or history lesson.

A teacher of English learners might use the frontloading vocabulary strategy. What does 'frontloading vocabulary' mean?

  1. Previewing key vocabulary before a lesson or presentation

  2. Using long, complicated words

  3. Using at least 50 new English words each lesson

  4. Requiring students to find at least two synonyms for each new word
