In group work, there is a social learning component in this aspect:

  1. students can monitor each other

  2. students must interact with each other

  3. students are obliged to be part of a group

  4. students want to get a good grade as a group

Caroline is a language learning teacher who always asks questions so her students can actively participate in class. As part of questioning required in class, Caroline should also:

  1. get student to write the questions for practice

  2. elaborate on the students' answers

  3. encourage students to ask questions

  4. get students to repeat the question she asks

Elaboration is a social learning strategy that requires students to:

  1. ask questions in response

  2. give simple answers to questions

  3. request clarification

  4. provide detailed answers

The following is an example of an activity in which students teach their peers:

  1. students make an oral presentation of a topic

  2. students read aloud

  3. students monitor each other in class

  4. students correct each other's mistakes

The following is an example of a social learning strategy a teacher applies in a language learning class:

  1. teacher introduces a grammar piece with examples

  2. teacher gives students a listening exercise

  3. teacher assigns a fill-in-the-blanks activity

  4. teacher assigns a group work activity
