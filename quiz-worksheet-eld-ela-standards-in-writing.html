In California, 'emerging, expanding and bridging' are:

  1. ELA/ELD frameworks

  2. English proficiency levels

  3. English proficiency standards

  4. ELA standards

In California, text types and purposes, production and distribution of writing, research to build and present knowledge, and range of writing are all:

  1. ELD standards

  2. ELA and ELD standards

  3. anchor standards for writing

  4. ELA standards

In California, English learners have to succeed in the following areas:

  1. English Language Development

  2. English Language Arts

  3. English Language Development and English Language Arts

  4. English for social purposes

The following expectation as per California writing standards is reasonable for a student in ninth grade:

  1. do sustained research for a project-based assignment

  2. gather data to use in writing

  3. use basic arguments to support claims

  4. use of basic writing conventions

A student who makes frequent errors in grammar and writing conventions that sometimes impedes meaning is, as per California English proficiency standards, at this level:

  1. K-5 grades

  2. expanding

  3. emerging

  4. bridging
