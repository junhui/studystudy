About This CTEL 1, 2, 3 Combined Exam: Course & Study Guide

This course and study guide is a compilation of video lessons and practice quizzes that align with the subjects that could appear on the CTEL 1, 2, 3 Combined exam. The study guide is taught by expert instructors who examine topics like the theories, processes, and stages of language acquisition; phonology; morphology; foundations of English language literacy; and language development approaches and methods. This course is self-paced and designed to meet your learning needs to prepare you for taking the CTEL 031/032/033 exam.

CTEL 1, 2, 3 Combined Exam Course Information

This course is designed to prepare you for taking the CTEL 1, 2, 3 Combined (031/032/033) test, covering all topics outlined in the official test materials provided by Pearson Education, Inc. Our professional instructors created this comprehensive study guide to help you master these objectives. Some of the topics covered here are listed below:

Language & Language Development

  • Demonstrate knowledge of phonology and morphology
  • Identify the synaptic classes, synaptic rules in English, and English sentence patterns
  • Demonstrate understanding of the different social functions of language
  • Apply strategies for promoting English learners' communicative competence
  • Recognize pragmatic features of oral and written languages that influence or convey meaning
  • Analyze the significance for teaching and learning contemporary theories of language acquisition
  • Understand the development of language for social and academic purposes
  • Analyze the pedagogical implications of cognitive, linguistic, and physical factors affecting the development of additional languages

Assessment & Instruction

  • Demonstrate knowledge of how the California English Language Development standards support the English Language Arts standards
  • Understanding of the role and purposes of assessment in programs for English learners
  • Demonstrate conceptual understanding and applied knowledge of how to interpret and use the assessment results of English learners
  • Identify the federal and state requirements for program implementation
  • Understand the components of effective instructional delivery in ELD and SDAIE
  • List the essential procedures used in planning SDAIE lessons

Culture & Inclusion

  • Demonstrate knowledge of practical applications of current research and research-based theories related to cultural factors
  • Differentiate the differences among various processes of cultural contact
  • Identify the crucial issues and challenges faced by culturally and linguistically diverse groups in California
  • Apply strategies for providing authentic opportunities for English learners to use the English language
  • Demonstrate understanding of culturally inclusive curriculum and instruction

Registering and Preparing for the CTEL 1, 2, 3 Combined Exam

Registering for this test requires that you create an account on the Commission on Teaching Credentialing (CTC) website and submit all requested information and payment of exam fees. Once your registration is processed, log in to your CTC account and click on 'Schedule.' Choose your test date and location. Once this is verified, you will receive a confirmation email from Pearson VUE, the administrator for this exam.

Ensure that you are fully prepared to sit for the CTEL Combined test by reviewing the information discussed in this study guide. It is made up of 29 chapters and 234 lessons that align with the topics covered on the exam. Short, engaging video and text lessons explore such topics as phonology and morphology; different social functions of language; how the California English Language Development standards support the English Language Arts standards; development of language for social and academic purposes; and the critical issues and challenges faced by culturally and linguistically diverse groups in California. Lessons take only 5-10 minutes to review and are always accessible from any device, making it convenient and easy to study this material whenever and wherever you can. Use self-assessment lesson quizzes and chapter exams to demonstrate how much information you have retained and whether you are ready to take this exam.

For more information on registering and preparing for the CTEL 031/032/033 exam, check out our CTEL Informational Resources.

  • Identify the federal and state requirements for program implementation
  • Understand the components of effective instructional delivery in ELD and SDAIE
  • List the essential procedures used in planning SDAIE lessons

Culture & Inclusion

  • Demonstrate knowledge of practical applications of current research and research-based theories related to cultural factors
  • Differentiate the differences among various processes of cultural contact
  • Identify the crucial issues and challenges faced by culturally and linguistically diverse groups in California
  • Apply strategies for providing authentic opportunities for English learners to use the English language
  • Demonstrate understanding of culturally inclusive curriculum and instruction

Prep & Register

Registering and Preparing for the CTEL 1, 2, 3 Combined Exam

Registering for this test requires that you create an account on the Commission on Teaching Credentialing (CTC) website and submit all requested information and payment of exam fees. Once your registration is processed, log in to your CTC account and click on 'Schedule.' Choose your test date and location. Once this is verified, you will receive a confirmation email from Pearson VUE, the administrator for this exam.

Ensure that you are fully prepared to sit for the CTEL Combined test by reviewing the information discussed in this study guide. It is made up of 29 chapters and 234 lessons that align with the topics covered on the exam. Short, engaging video and text lessons explore such topics as phonology and morphology; different social functions of language; how the California English Language Development standards support the English Language Arts standards; development of language for social and academic purposes; and the critical issues and challenges faced by culturally and linguistically diverse groups in California. Lessons take only 5-10 minutes to review and are always accessible from any device, making it convenient and easy to study this material whenever and wherever you can. Use self-assessment lesson quizzes and chapter exams to demonstrate how much information you have retained and whether you are ready to take this exam.

For more information on registering and preparing for the CTEL 031/032/033 exam, check out our CTEL Informational Resources.
