María is a student from El Salvador who struggles with the terminology in math, science, and other subjects. This is probably because María:

  1. has a learning disability

  2. has no ability to learn new languages

  3. doesn't study hard enough

  4. has low English proficiency

Stefan is the only student from Germany in a school in the US. Despite the fact he is fully proficient in English, he appears not to have any friends in school. This factor could be preventing Stefan from fully adjusting to his school:

  1. Stefan does not like classroom activities

  2. Stefan is used to different assessment methods

  3. Stefan doesn't identify with the school population

  4. Stefan feels homesick

Classroom activities can prevent foreign students from culturally adjusting, especially if the activities:

  1. involve cultural components

  2. require students to work in groups

  3. are not fun

  4. are not translated into the student's native language

Marcela is an Ecuadorian student who has never taken a standardized test because the assessment methods in Ecuador's educational system are different from those used in the US. The school could help Marcela by:

  1. giving her the type of tests she is used to taking

  2. giving Marcela standardized tests in Spanish

  3. advising Marcela's parents to find a school similar to the one in Ecuador

  4. guiding Marcela on strategies to take standardized tests

A foreign student arrives at a school and during the first three months feels homesick, has low grades, and isolates from other students. All these are signs of:

  1. lack of desire to integrate in the new culture

  2. culture shock

  3. poor quality of education back home

  4. not able to overcome cultural adjustment
