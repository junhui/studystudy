How does a think-aloud help students with reading comprehension?

  1. It gives everyone in the class, including English Language Learners, the chance to read aloud.

  2. It allows teachers to share their thoughts, beliefs, and opinions with their students.

  3. It gives English Language Learners the opportunity to practice their presentation skills.

  4. It teaches English Language Learners how to read fluently and interact with the text.

Why is it OK to allow beginner English learners to read texts in their native language?

  1. The research shows that trying to teach English Language Learners how to read in a second language is not effective.

  2. Learning how to read in English is not as much of a priority as learning how to listen and speak in English.

  3. Students who are literate in their native language are more successful with second language acquisition than those who are not.

  4. You don't want them to forget their native language.

What kind of learning environment is most conducive to second language acquisition?

  1. A welcoming environment where the teacher reduces student pressure by assuring them that mistakes are part of the learning process.

  2. An environment where the teacher has high expectations and encourages students to strive for perfection.

  3. A noisy environment where English Language Learners can listen to their peers' conversations.

  4. A quiet environment where students work independently 100% of the time.

You are working with a group of students that includes English Language Learners and native English speakers. Which of the following is an appropriate task for a beginning English Language Learner during group work?

  1. Draw a picture to represent some aspect of the text.

  2. Write a summary of the text.

  3. Facilitate a group discussion about the text.

  4. Record all questions that students in the group have about the text.

Why are sentence frames a good writing strategy for English Language Learners?

  1. They teach students how to improve their handwriting.

  2. They show students how to write sentences while also providing necessary instructional support.

  3. They allow students to practice writing in their native language.

  4. They allow students to get away with doing the bare minimum in their classes.
