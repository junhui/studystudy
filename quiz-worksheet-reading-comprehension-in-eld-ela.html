A California teacher assigns students to discuss the topic 'The way I celebrate Halloween' after students read an article on Halloween traditions. This exercise aims at students doing the following:

  1. relating to the text

  2. evaluating the author's credibility

  3. making inferences

  4. drawing conclusions

When a teacher asks students to tell whether the language in a text is casual, formal, or technical, students have to identify this aspect of the text:

  1. structures

  2. comprehension

  3. rhetorical devices

  4. features

To practice comprehension of a text when a teacher works with second grade students in California, the ideal questions are:

  1. those that trigger abstract thinking

  2. those that begin with 'what' or 'who'

  3. those that are yes/no

  4. those that get the student locating the answer in the text

The abilities to predict the content in a text, compare information from different sources, and answer essential questions about a text, are all part of this skill:

  1. reading as English learners

  2. reading with appropriate intonation

  3. reading accurately

  4. reading comprehension

The ability to evaluate how well writers use language to present ideas is part of this skill:

  1. differentiating between fact and opinion

  2. making inferences

  3. reading comprehension

  4. finding rhetorical devices
